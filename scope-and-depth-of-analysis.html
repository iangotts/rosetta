Summary

609 documents across four product areas were collected, cross-referenced and checked for conflicts. The work produced a 7,197-row dependency table, a 65-item conflicts register, 29 documented gaps and a searchable impact finder.

The analysis ran from 25 to 28 September 2026. It covered Help Agent (Casey), Agentforce Voice, Data 360 (also known as Data Cloud, Genie or CDP) and the Well-Architected framework. It included Salesforce's official documentation, field-engineering (FDE) guidance, a Salesforce whitepaper, and a product naming reference plus glossary supplied for this work.

Naming and terminology

One alias dictionary treats every name for the same product or concept as one thing. It merges three sources into 187 groups that actually occur in the documents.

Source What it contributed
Business terminology and acronyms (D360) spreadsheet 43 product families with 635 alias rows; 501 glossary terms, of which 71 are Salesforce or Data 360 specific (DMO, DLO, data stream, calculated insights, zero copy and others)
Product naming reference v12 About 75 product rows, with the name each Salesforce property uses, plus stale and legacy names
Renames found during the analysis About 30 groups, e.g. Genie / CDP / Data Cloud → Data 360, Einstein Studio → AI Models, topics → subagents, Casey → Help Agent, Messaging for In-App and Web → Enhanced Chat

The dictionary also maps 26 old or divergent names to their current names. Four are provisional because they rest on pre-Dreamforce guidance: Agentforce Service → Service Cloud, Agentforce Sales → Sales Cloud, Agentforce 360 Platform → Salesforce Platform, and Customer 360 Platform → Salesforce Platform.

It was used in five places:

  • Aligning text before comparison, so copied passages match across renames
  • Linking pages that define a concept to pages that rely on it
  • Flagging pages and screenshots that still use old names
  • Comparing facts such as editions, limits and billing per concept rather than per name
  • Expanding searches in the impact finder

Ambiguous short acronyms (ID, Org, SI, TE) and generic IT terms (API, JSON) help align text but are never used to link documents.

Conflict and gap analysis

Five passes, each wider than the last, produced 65 confirmed conflicts and 29 gaps. Every conflict was checked against the source text of both documents before it was recorded; candidates that turned out to be consistent, expected or boilerplate were set aside with a reason.

Pass Date Screened Added
1. Targeted review: terms, numbers, limits, billing and editions, read by hand 25 Sep 345 documents 25 conflicts, 25 gaps
2. Whole-folder rerun with Data 360, the naming reference and the Grown-Up's Guide 27 Sep 540 documents 14 conflicts, 4 gaps; 3 candidates dropped after checking
3. Alias rerun: editions, billing, limits and dates compared per concept 28 Sep 18 disagreeing fact groups 3 conflicts; 6 existing rows strengthened
4. Sentence drift: near-identical sentences that differ in numbers, dates, editions, negation, Beta/GA or requirements 28 Sep 36,150 sentences, 47,930 pairs, 714 differing, 142 read by hand 2 conflicts; 33 wording items grouped into 11 rows
5. Implementation advice: opposite or different recommendations on the same subject 28 Sep 3,082 recommendations from 326 documents; 114 opposite-direction pairs, plus the FDE and Guide advice read topic by topic 10 conflicts

Of the 714 drift candidates, 629 were release-note template lines for different features, 31 boilerplate and 35 wording or scope differences. All 714 are in DOC_drift_candidates.csv with a triage label.

Limits

The analysis is broad but not exhaustive. These are the known boundaries and how to close them.

  • Data 360 scope. The folder holds the 251 pages relevant to implementing Help Agent, not all of Data 360: about 1,200 other Help pages and about 4,000 data-model reference pages were left out.
  • Web pages. 35 of the 69 were checked on title and summary only: 23 legacy WA Tools pages block access, some are forms or videos, and two Trailhead links now return 404.
  • Images from architect.salesforce.com. 124 occurrences could only be analysed from alt text and captions. Text recognition can also miss or misread very small diagram labels.
  • Implementation advice. Advice was read topic by topic for voice design, model choice, greetings, escalation, caller verification, testing, grounding and asynchronous processing. Detailed Data 360 connector configuration and WAF security patterns were not read at that depth.
  • Aliases. A feature without an alias group, such as Data Cloud One, is matched by its exact name only. Team shorthand such as DC1 can be added.
  • Provisional names. Four canonical names rest on pre-Dreamforce presenter guidance and should be confirmed after the event.
  • Point in time. Everything reflects the documents as saved between 25 and 28 September 2026. The dependency table, register and impact finder need a rebuild when documents change, and the rebuild scripts can be rerun.
