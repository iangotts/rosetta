Executive Summary
Fill every study on time, keep every sponsor informed, and earn the next phase — with a process we can defend.

Cal Testing runs clinical trials on behalf of tier 1 pharmaceutical sponsors. We win that work study by study, and we keep it by enrolling the right participants on time and reporting honestly on progress. Today the commercial side of the business runs on Salesforce, but the trial itself runs on a few simple custom objects, site spreadsheets, a Mailchimp audience and the telephone.

This initiative funds a rebuild of the clinical trials process on Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud, with agents supporting each stage: winning the study, finding and contacting potential participants, enrolling and retaining them, returning results to the sponsor, and winning the next phase. Qualified people continue to make every clinical and eligibility decision.

"Sponsors do not award Phase 2 to the organisation with the best proposal. They award it to the one that enrolled Phase 1 on time and told them the truth along the way."

The Problem We Are Solving
Our sales process is in Salesforce. Our trials are in spreadsheets.

A person who signed up on our website for a diabetes study two years ago, was called by a recruiter, screen-failed at one of our sites, and later unsubscribed from our emails exists in four places — as a Salesforce lead among our pharmaceutical prospects, as a trial participant record, as a line in a site spreadsheet, and as a Mailchimp subscriber — with no single identity and no dated record of what they agreed to. We cannot see them as one person, so we cannot be sure we are entitled to contact them, or whether they are a good match for the next study.

The result: recruiters call widely and screen-fail often, enrollment forecasts given to sponsors are estimates, status reports are assembled by hand, and business development learns about a sponsor's next phase late. Because the data is personal and much of it is health information, every one of these gaps is also a compliance exposure under HIPAA and California privacy law.

Technology Foundation
We keep the commercial core. We replace what the trial runs on.

Cal Testing's sales process on Salesforce works and stays. The custom trial objects, the spreadsheets and the unlogged outreach are replaced by a standard clinical data model. The sponsor's data capture system remains the clinical system of record. Our IT team configures Salesforce but does not develop; delivery will be led by a systems integrator.

Agentforce Sales (Sales Cloud) — in place
Sponsor leads, contacts, accounts and opportunities
Life Sciences Cloud — new
Studies, sites, participants, candidate matching, enrollment and visits; replaces the custom trial objects
Data 360 & Agentforce — new
One governed participant profile with consent status, and the six agents that act on it
Participant & Sponsor Portals — new
Sign-up, pre-screening, electronic consent and visit schedule for participants; status and released results for sponsors
Mailchimp — under review
Not suitable for participant health information; to be replaced for participant messaging, decision open for the sponsor newsletter
Sponsor EDC & Central Laboratory — external
Clinical data and test results, connected through MuleSoft; not replaced
