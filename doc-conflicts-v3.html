Baseline tensions (not counted above)

Lessons Learnt (LL) and the Well-Architected (WAF) docs, including the Data 360 pages duplicated in data360-docs, were both assumed correct, but they disagree with each other or themselves here. Resolve these before using either to settle a conflict.

  • before_reasoning frequency. WAF (hybrid reasoning with Agent Script) says before_reasoning runs on every parse, including after each tool call, and warns against transitions inside it. LL says it runs once per entry per turn and not after tool-batch re-resolution; LL R10/B3 use an unconditional transition in before_reasoning.
  • after_reasoning reliability. WAF says it is reliable except when is_displayable: True exits the loop. LL says it is skipped on a transition, a cancelled turn and tool calls.
  • Golden record. Data 360 Architecture (169) says a unified profile "isn't a golden record"; Integration Patterns (173) and Document AI (174) use "golden record(s)" for it.
  • Document AI PII masking. 174 excludes multimodal documents from masking, yet also says "The ETL satisfies this natively", and elsewhere says to apply masking after extraction.
  • Document AI model list. 174 says "Gemini and additional models are being planned" and also lists "(GPT-4o, Gemini, or Claude)" as available.
  • File federation formats. 169 says Parquet plus Iceberg; 173 says "Iceberg or Delta Lake".
  • Companion-org activation. Provisioning (012) shows activation as supported (✅) in its table, but its text says "In the future vision, they can also access activation features."
